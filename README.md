
# Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis | Excel

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Zomato restaurant data using Microsoft Excel to identify restaurant distribution,
customer preferences, pricing patterns, ratings, and online delivery trends. The project aims to generate data-driven 
insights to support restaurant expansion and business decision-making.

## Objectives
- Analyze restaurant distribution across countries and cities.
- Identify popular cuisines and restaurant categories.
- Evaluate restaurant ratings and customer votes.
- Analyze price ranges and online delivery trends.
- Identify potential locations for restaurant expansion.
- Generate actionable business insights using Excel.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Formulas
- Charts and Visualizations
- Interactive Dashboard

## Key Analysis Performed
- Country-wise and city-wise restaurant distribution.
- Cuisine-wise restaurant analysis.
- Restaurant ratings and votes analysis.
- Price range and average cost analysis.
- Online delivery and table booking analysis.
- Objective and subjective business analysis.
- Dashboard creation for key performance indicators and trends.

## Key Insights
- Identified countries and cities with a higher concentration of restaurants.
- Analyzed popular cuisines and restaurant distribution patterns.
- Evaluated customer ratings and voting patterns.
- Examined the relationship between price ranges and restaurant ratings.
- Analyzed online delivery availability across restaurants.
- Generated recommendations for potential market expansion and business growth.

## Dashboard
An Excel dashboard was developed to present key metrics and restaurant trends through visualizations, 
helping users understand the analysis and business insights.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates practical Excel skills, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, dashboard creation, and business problem-solving. The analysis translates restaurant data into meaningful insights to support market expansion, customer preference analysis, and strategic decision-making.
